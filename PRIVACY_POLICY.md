# Privacy Policy for Flow AI Keyboard

**Last updated:** November 2026

Flow AI Keyboard ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android keyboard application handles your data when you use our services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

* **Voice / Microphone Data:**
  When you tap the Microphone button on the keyboard, the app temporarily captures your spoken voice input to convert Hindi speech into text and translate it into English.
  * **Audio is NOT stored or recorded permanently.** Audio processing is ephemeral and immediately discarded once speech transcription is completed.
* **Text Input:**
  When you tap the "Translate" or "Polish" buttons, the text you specifically choose to translate is sent securely via encrypted HTTPS to the translation engine (Google Gemini API) to generate the English translation and fix grammar.
  * **We do NOT log, store, or monitor your keystrokes.**
  * Passwords, credit card details, and personal conversations are never logged or stored.

---

### 2. Permissions Used

* **`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`**:
  Used solely for the voice-to-text translation feature when the user explicitly taps the microphone button.
* **`android.permission.INTERNET`**:
  Used solely to communicate with Google Gemini API for real-time translation and grammar correction.
* **`android.permission.VIBRATE`**:
  Used strictly for optional haptic feedback on keypresses.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services

* **Google Gemini API**:
  Translation and grammar correction requests are processed using Google's Generative AI services. Google processes this data in accordance with Google's API Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Security

* **No Local Storage of Personal Text:** We do not save your messages, search queries, or voice recordings to any database or local file.
* **Encryption in Transit:** All API requests are encrypted using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS encryption.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
